# Sidedoor: what it is and what it costs

Source: sidedoorjobs.app (homepage, pricing page and FAQ), as live on 2026-10-25. Only Sidedoor's own voice may
use this file (the TikTok closing slide, the caption's brand line). If the site changes, update this file first.

## What it does

- Sidedoor helps you land your next job, or your first. It finds people at the companies you want, gets an email
  you can trust, and drafts a note built on what you have in common. You press send.
- How it works:
  1. **Tell us about you.** Add your resume and answer a few questions about the industries, roles and companies
     you're after. Optional; the more you add, the better the suggestions.
  2. **Find people who fit.** Get suggestions from your resume and goals, or search by company, role, school or
     past employer. You see each person's title, company and what you share. **Searching is free.**
  3. **Unlock their name and email.** A credit unlocks a person's name and work email, with an honest label, and
     a ready draft to open in Gmail. Read it, edit it, send it.
- **Real common ground:** search results show who went to the same school as you or worked at the same company.
- **A draft, ready in Gmail:** a short email built on what you share; open it in Gmail or your email app, edit, send.
- **Replies stay high:** each person is unlocked by only a few people a month, so the people you write to aren't
  buried in the same request.
- **A bounce is covered:** if an email bounces because the address doesn't exist, the credit comes back
  automatically.
- **Your outreach, tracked:** unlocked, sent, replied, bounced, all in one place, exportable.
- **You send every email yourself.** Sidedoor never connects to your inbox, never sends mail for you, and has no
  open or click tracking.
- **Any industry.** Banking, consulting, law, policy and tech are the examples it knows best. Coverage is
  strongest at mid-size and large employers.
- **Students:** whether you're after an internship, your first job or your next one, the people who can tell you
  how they got in are the same.

## Email labels

- **Verified:** a direct check confirmed this mailbox exists.
- **Likely:** follows the email format confirmed for people who work there.
- **Best guess:** the address Sidedoor's data sources point to for this person.

## Prices (US dollars)

- **Free:** 3 credits to start.
- **Pro:** $12.99 a month for 20 credits a month.
- **Max:** $19.99 a month for 50 credits a month.
- **10-credit pack:** $9.99, one time; pack credits never expire.
- One credit unlocks one person (name, work email with its label, and a draft). If no email is found, or it
  bounces because the address doesn't exist, you aren't charged.

## Never say

- That Sidedoor gets anyone hired, gets interviews, or raises reply rates by any amount.
- That every address is verified.
- That Sidedoor sends emails, reads inboxes or tracks opens.
